iCRM is Gagstek's flagship sales platform — taking your team from lead generation through pipeline, quotes, digital contracts, and recurring invoices in one unified workspace.

What is iCRM?

Your Sales Team's Complete Workspace

iCRM is the valued, client-oriented sales software built to boost your revenue. From auto-generated leads to a full pipeline of forecasts and won opportunities — every step of your sales journey lives in one place.

Designed by former Management & Technology Consulting executives, iCRM eliminates data silos and gives your team a modern interface with task management, activity tracking, and real-time collaboration baked in.

Gain a clear view of your opportunity pipeline with a drag-and-drop board. Customize stages for each sales team, analyze lost opportunities, and log every interaction instantly within the opportunity's timeline.

Getting Started

Up & Running in Three Steps

Deploying iCRM is fast, guided, and tailored to your team's workflow from day one.

1

Connect & Configure

Install iCRM via AppExchange or Gagstek's onboarding link. Approve integrations, configure your sales stages, and map your existing data to the platform in minutes.

2

Import & Activate

Bring in your existing leads, accounts, and opportunities. Activate email routing, set up VoIP telephony, and sync with Google Calendar or Outlook for immediate productivity.

3

Sell & Scale

Start managing your pipeline, creating quotes, and tracking performance through real-time dashboards. As your team grows, iCRM scales effortlessly across regions and teams.
