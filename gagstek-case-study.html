Overview

A mid-sized steel fabrication and structural engineering company operating across Victoria's industrial corridor had been struggling with disconnected sales processes, manual job quoting, and no visibility into project delivery timelines or client history.

Gagstek was engaged to conduct a full systems audit, design a tailored Salesforce implementation across their sales and operations teams, and deploy AI-driven automation to eliminate manual bottlenecks — restoring commercial confidence and enabling the business to scale without adding headcount.

Challenges & Objectives
No Centralised Sales Pipeline
Quotes, client communications, and job statuses were tracked across spreadsheets, email threads, and whiteboards. Sales managers had no unified view of where each opportunity stood, causing deals to go cold unnoticed.
Manual Quoting Costing Hours Daily
Every quote was built from scratch in Word or Excel, with no template standardisation or pricing logic. The estimating team spent 3–4 hours per day on document formatting rather than high-value work.
ERP and CRM Completely Disconnected
The company's job management ERP and customer records existed in entirely separate systems. Data was duplicated, outdated, or missing — making it impossible to link client history to active jobs or invoices.
Zero Reporting or Executive Visibility
Leadership had no dashboards, no weekly pipeline reporting, and no way to forecast revenue. Decisions were made on gut feel with no data to support or challenge them.
Our Solution
Salesforce Sales Cloud Implementation
  • Full CRM build with custom objects for jobs, quotes, and client sites
  • Pipeline stages configured to match the company's actual sales process
  • Automated follow-up tasks and opportunity reminders deployed from Day 1
Automated Quoting Engine
  • CPQ-style quoting workflow built inside Salesforce with templated pricing rules
  • Quote generation time reduced from 3 hours to under 20 minutes per job
  • PDF output with branding auto-generated and emailed directly from CRM
ERP & Systems Integration
  • Bi-directional API integration connecting Salesforce to their job management ERP
  • Single source of truth for all client, job, and invoice data established
  • Real-time sync eliminating duplicate data entry across both platforms
Executive Reporting & AI Insights
  • Live dashboards delivering pipeline value, win rate, and revenue forecast
  • Agentic AI surfacing at-risk deals and recommending follow-up actions
  • Weekly automated report delivered to leadership every Monday morning
