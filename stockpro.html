What is StockPro?

Inventory Intelligence for Modern Operations

StockPro is Gagstek's dedicated inventory management system — built to give your warehouse and operations teams point-in-time visibility across every product, location, and delivery route.

Deeply integrated with iCRM, StockPro connects your sales and supply chain in a single workflow — from the moment a quote is created to the final delivery confirmation, with zero tool-switching.

Real-time tracking across multiple warehouses, automated replenishment, GS1 barcode scanning, and intelligent routing — everything your team needs to run a lean, responsive inventory operation.

Getting Started

From Setup to Full Control

StockPro is designed to go live fast and integrate seamlessly with your existing operations and iCRM account.

1

Configure Your Warehouses

Define your warehouse locations, storage zones, and product categories. Set up push/pull routing rules and barcode formats — ready to scan in minutes with no heavy setup required.

2

Import Your Inventory

Bulk-import your existing product catalog with opening stock levels, supplier info, and reorder thresholds. StockPro validates and syncs everything automatically.

3

Connect to iCRM

Activate the iCRM integration so your sales team can instantly check availability, trigger orders, and track deliveries directly from their opportunity records.

4

Operate & Optimize

Start scanning barcodes, processing shipments, and monitoring KPIs. Automated replenishment rules handle reordering while your dashboards surface insights in real time.
