The Challenge

When General iPaaS Hits Its Limits

The client had previously attempted integrations using general iPaaS platforms such as Workato and Boomi. While effective for SaaS-to-SaaS automation, the environment exposed critical gaps when scaled to a B2B-heavy, EDI-driven ecosystem.

  • Frequent onboarding of new trading partners creating recurring engineering bottlenecks
  • Complex EDI standards requiring custom handling beyond standard connectors
  • High transaction volumes with direct operational dependency on integration reliability
  • Escalating licensing costs tied to usage and message volume
  • Heavy reliance on engineering teams for monitoring and issue resolution

The client needed an integration platform designed specifically for B2B, EDI, and partner-centric operations — not a general-purpose automation tool repurposed for enterprise complexity.

Gagstek's Recommendation

A Purpose-Built B2B Integration Backbone

After delivering integrations across multiple platforms, Gagstek recommended the Lobster Data Platform as the strategic backbone for the client's integration architecture.

The decision was driven by Lobster's B2B-first design, operational visibility, and predictable cost model — aligned with the client's long-term scale requirements. Unlike general iPaaS tools that bolt on EDI support, Lobster was engineered from the ground up for the complexity of trading partner ecosystems.

Solution Architecture

Lobster at the Core of the Integration Landscape

Gagstek designed and implemented a fully integrated landscape with Lobster as the central hub, connecting SAP ERP, Salesforce, and 40+ external trading partners.

Native EDI Processing
Full support for EDIFACT, X12, and partner-specific flat files — no custom workarounds needed.
SAP & Salesforce Integration
Standardized, reusable mappings connecting ERP and CRM with minimal maintenance overhead.
Template-Driven Partner Onboarding
Accelerated partner go-lives through reusable templates, reducing setup time dramatically.
Centralized Monitoring
Operations teams gained self-service message monitoring and reprocessing without engineering involvement.
Clear Role Separation
Business operations and engineering workflows cleanly separated, reducing cross-team friction.
Reduced Complexity
A unified architecture replacing fragmented point-to-point integrations across the enterprise.
