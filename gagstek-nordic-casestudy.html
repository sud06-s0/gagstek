Overview

A leading Nordic automotive manufacturer, operating across Scandinavia with a dealer network spanning three countries, faced escalating pressure from digital-native competitors. Legacy CRM systems, manual quoting workflows, and siloed production data were compressing margins and slowing deal velocity.

Gagstek was engaged as the Cloud & AI Systems integration partner to modernise their sales infrastructure, automate cross-functional workflows, and build a unified data layer — turning operational complexity into measurable competitive advantage.

Challenges & Objectives
Fragmented Sales Pipeline

Dealer networks across three countries operated on disconnected spreadsheets and a decade-old CRM, causing quote delays of up to 11 business days and a 28% deal-loss rate attributable to slow response time.

Manual Production Forecasting

Demand signals were relayed from sales to manufacturing via email, leading to inventory misalignment, delayed builds, and excess carrying costs estimated at €1.4M annually.

Weak Aftersales Engagement

No structured lifecycle touchpoints post-delivery meant poor service contract renewal rates and missed upsell revenue across an existing fleet of 12,000+ vehicles.

Data Locked in Silos

Finance, logistics, and CRM data lived in three separate systems with no unified reporting, making real-time business intelligence impossible at the executive level.

Gagstek's Solution
Salesforce Sales Cloud & CPQ
  • Unified dealer portal with real-time pipeline visibility
  • Automated CPQ reducing quote time from 11 days to 4 hours
  • Custom Lightning components for vehicle configuration
AI-Powered Demand Forecasting
  • AI workflows connecting CRM pipeline to ERP inventory
  • Predictive build-schedule triggers based on deal signals
  • Zero manual handoffs between sales and manufacturing
Aftersales & Customer Automation
  • WhatsApp & SMS bots for automated service reminders
  • AI-driven service contract renewal campaigns
  • 360° customer view with full vehicle ownership history
Unified Analytics & Reporting
  • Salesforce dashboards aggregating ERP, CRM & logistics
  • Automated executive KPI reports delivered daily

Conclusion

This Nordic automotive manufacturer's transformation demonstrates how a disciplined Cloud & AI integration strategy creates measurable value at every stage of the value chain. By combining Salesforce's enterprise CRM capabilities with Gagstek's AI-first integration approach, the business moved from reactive operations to a proactive, data-driven model — positioning itself as a digital leader in the Nordic automotive market. The engagement continues with a second phase targeting connected-vehicle data and predictive maintenance services.
