Overview

A mid-sized industrial manufacturer based in Central Europe — operating across Germany, Poland, and the Netherlands — was struggling to act on its own data. Despite years of Salesforce investment, customer and operational data sat fragmented across multiple clouds and legacy ERPs, making unified intelligence impossible and AI-driven automation out of reach.

Gagstek was engaged to unify the client's data estate on Salesforce Data Cloud and deploy Agentforce — a suite of autonomous AI agents — to automate service resolution, sales follow-up, and field operations, creating measurable ROI within the first two quarters of go-live.

Challenges & Objectives
Data Fragmented Across 5 Systems

Customer records, order history, warranty data, and service tickets lived in separate systems — Salesforce CRM, SAP ERP, a legacy ticketing tool, and two regional databases — with no unified customer profile available to any team in real time.

High Volume of Manual Service Tasks

The service team was handling over 2,400 inbound B2B service queries per month manually — from order status checks to warranty validations — consuming significant agent capacity that should have been directed at complex escalations.

Missed Sales Follow-Up Opportunities

Sales reps were missing renewal windows and reorder signals due to no unified view of buying behaviour. An estimated 18% of reorder revenue was being lost annually to competitors responding faster.

No Actionable AI Foundation

The client wanted to leverage Salesforce AI capabilities but lacked the unified, clean data layer required for Agentforce to function — making any AI deployment impossible without first resolving the data architecture.

Gagstek's Solution
Salesforce Data Cloud Implementation
  • Unified customer data from SAP, Salesforce CRM, and 3 legacy sources
  • Real-time unified customer profiles with full purchase & service history
  • MuleSoft integration layer for live ERP-to-Data Cloud sync
  • GDPR-compliant data governance and consent management configured
Agentforce Service Agent Deployment
  • Autonomous AI agent handling order status, warranty checks & FAQs
  • Grounded on Data Cloud unified profiles for accurate, contextual responses
  • Seamless human handoff for complex cases with full context transfer
Agentforce Sales Development Agent
  • AI agent monitoring reorder signals and renewal windows from Data Cloud
  • Automated personalised outreach triggered on buying behaviour patterns
  • Sales reps alerted only when human engagement is required
Analytics & Einstein Insights
  • Real-time executive dashboards powered by Data Cloud segments
  • Predictive churn and reorder scoring surfaced inside Sales Cloud

Conclusion

This engagement demonstrates that Agentforce is only as powerful as the data it is grounded on. By first building a robust, unified data estate on Salesforce Data Cloud — connecting SAP, CRM, and legacy systems — Gagstek created the foundation for AI agents that could act with genuine accuracy and context. The result was not just automation, but autonomous intelligence: agents that resolve service queries, identify sales opportunities, and surface insights without human intervention. For this European manufacturer, the combination of Data Cloud and Agentforce has fundamentally shifted how the business operates — from reactive, manual processes to a proactive, AI-first model that scales across borders.
