Project Overview

A Digital Transformation for Credit Operations

A major financial institution in the APAC region partnered with Gagstek to modernize its digital credit processing platform. The client sought to improve operational efficiency, strengthen customer engagement, and establish a centralized ecosystem for employees, brokers, and customers.

The solution automated the generation of all key insurance documents — including contract renewals, policy endorsements, and complex amendments — through scalable workflows supporting both batch automation and interactive editing.

Over an 8-month development period, 25 templates were created ranging from simple notifications to intricate policy schedules. Of these, 22 templates are now live in production, with additional ones under development as business requirements evolve.

Key Business Challenges

Five Friction Points Slowing Down the Institution

  • Disconnected systems and limited data visibility across departments and regions
  • Manual processes affecting data quality and introducing operational errors
  • Slow, paper-driven document collection creating bottlenecks across teams
  • Limited customer self-service options, increasing support load
  • Inconsistent engagement and communication with brokers and customers

Gagstek's Solution

An End-to-End Salesforce Transformation

Gagstek developed a comprehensive Salesforce transformation roadmap and delivered a fully integrated platform serving customers, employees, and brokers from a single centralized system.

Transformation Roadmap
Developed an end-to-end Salesforce transformation roadmap aligned with the client's operational goals and regulatory requirements.
Experience Cloud Portal
Built a centralized Experience Cloud portal providing tailored access for customers, employees, and brokers — all from one platform.
Automated Workflows
Automated workflows and business rules to improve data accuracy, reduce manual effort, and enforce consistent processes across the organization.
Lightning Web Components
Implemented responsive LWC for a modern, high-performance user experience across desktop and mobile interfaces.
Document Automation
Integrated Conga and SheetJS for automated generation of policy documents, renewals, endorsements, and complex amendments at scale.
Real-Time Communication
Enabled real-time communication and event-driven processes through REST APIs and Platform Events across all system touchpoints.
