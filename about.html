About Us

Your Partner in Digital Transformation

Gagstek is a Trusted Partner to some of the most recognized brands and industry leaders today as well as evolving digital players that are fast changing traditional industries.

We work in close partnership with our clients to deliver meaningful change and lasting impact. Our seasoned industry veterans are experienced in solving your business problems with the help of IT and automation.

We focus on providing Strategic Consulting on a wide range of Business & Technology concerns like Automation & Innovation, as well as effective Delivery of Solutions & Services that helps our clients achieve outcomes. We stay with our clients until the end customer is delighted and satisfied.

Gagstek was founded by former Management & Technology Consulting Executives as an alternative to traditional Big-4 consulting to deliver better value to clients. The founding leads not only possess deep technology and architecture skills but also have helped hundreds of clients in overcoming business problems through digitization.

Our strength is in delivering outstanding client experiences with a consulting model based on superior business & technology talent.

Our Journey

Building Gagstek,
Year by Year

From a vision to a global consulting firm — here is how we grew.

2023

Founded in Pune, India

Gagstek was founded by former Management & Technology Consulting Executives as an alternative to traditional Big-4 consulting. Core services launched: Salesforce, Java & Python Engineering.

2024

Global Expansion

Expanded to United States and Australia offices. Grew our certified Salesforce and AI team. Successfully delivered 100+ client engagements across multiple industries.

2025

AI & Agentic Solutions

Launched Agentic AI practice, iCRM product, and StockPro. Deepened partnerships across Salesforce, AWS, and Databricks ecosystems. Team of 50+ certified professionals.

2026

Scaling New Heights

Continuing to scale our team, deepen our technology expertise, and expand our client base across 12+ industries globally — staying ahead of digital transformation.
