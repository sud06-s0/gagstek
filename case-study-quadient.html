Project Overview

Automating Business-Critical Insurance Documents

The project focused on designing and developing business-critical insurance documents for Marsh Insurance customers in the New Zealand region using Quadient Inspire technology.

The solution automated the generation of all key insurance documents — including contract renewals, policy endorsements, and complex amendments — through scalable workflows supporting both batch automation and interactive editing.

Over an 8-month period, 25 templates were developed, ranging from simple notifications to intricate policy schedules. Out of these, 22 templates are live in production, with additional ones under development as business requirements evolve.

Automation was achieved through Inspire Scaler for batch processing and Inspire Interactive for user-driven customization — streamlining operations, enhancing document accuracy, ensuring compliance with regional regulations, and reducing turnaround time across multiple departments.

Business Challenges

Four Pain Points Slowing Marsh's Document Operations

  • Manual generation of policy and renewal documents led to inconsistent formatting and longer turnaround times
  • Frequent last-minute regulatory updates required repetitive rework across templates
  • Lack of a centralized document framework made it difficult to maintain branding consistency
  • Manual review cycles resulted in increased human error and compliance risks across policy outputs

The Solution

A Quadient Inspire-Based CCM Framework

To address these challenges, the team implemented a Quadient Inspire-based Customer Communication Management (CCM) framework that automated document creation end-to-end.

Modular Template Architecture
Designed a modular base template architecture for shared resources, ensuring faster template creation and easy maintenance across all product lines.
25 Dynamic Templates
Developed 25 dynamic templates for different insurance products — from simple notices to detailed policy schedules — with 22 now live in production.
Conditional Logic & Field Mapping
Implemented conditional logic and dynamic field mapping to personalize content for individual customers at scale.
Inspire Scaler + Interactive
Integrated Inspire Scaler for batch automation and Inspire Interactive for on-demand document editing by business teams.
Seamless Migration & Testing
Ensured smooth migration and testing workflows across Development, QA, and Production environments before every rollout.
End-to-End Architecture
Policy data feeds into Inspire Designer → Scaler for batch generation → Interactive for real-time editing → output as PDF, print stream, or email.

Role & Responsibilities

What the Gagstek Team Delivered

  • Requirement Analysis: Collaborated with business teams to gather template specifications, including input fields, data mapping, document structure, and branding elements
  • Template Architecture: Designed the base framework and reusable components — logos, footers, tables, and condition blocks
  • Development: Built and tested templates using Quadient Inspire Designer, including conditional data layouts and reusable masters
  • Testing & QA: Performed unit testing and validation before migrating templates to higher environments
  • Collaboration: Worked closely with Marsh's operations and compliance teams to incorporate feedback and ensure business readiness
  • Optimization: Enhanced template performance by minimizing resource redundancy and standardizing data mappings
