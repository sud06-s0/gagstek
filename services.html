Common FAQs

Have questions before you get started? We've answered the most common ones below.

How long does a Salesforce implementation take?
Rapid launch projects can go from kickoff to go-live in 4–6 weeks. Timelines scale with complexity — larger implementations with custom integrations typically run 2–5 months. Our sales team provides a precise timeline based on your specific requirements during the Assessment phase.
What does a Salesforce implementation cost?
Costs depend on scope, complexity, and the modules involved. All pricing is transparent and scoped upfront during our Assessment phase — no hidden fees, no surprise invoices. Contact us for a free scoping call and we'll give you a clear estimate.
Do we need to prepare anything before starting?
It helps to consolidate your existing data in one place and align your team on key business processes. The more clarity you have on how your business operates, the faster we can design and build. Don't worry — we guide you through everything in the Discovery phase.
How do you ensure solutions are customised to our needs?
Every engagement includes dedicated design sessions with our certified architects and delivery team. We blueprint your unique business challenges, needs, and proposed solutions — and you sign off on the design before we build a single thing.
What happens after go-live?
We provide hands-on user training so your team is fully equipped to manage the system. We also offer flexible Support & Managed Services plans for ongoing maintenance, enhancements, and expert help — whenever you need it.
Can Gagstek help with AI services even if we don't use Salesforce?
Absolutely. Our AI services — including sales automation, conversational AI, workflow orchestration, and custom AI product builds — are platform-agnostic. We integrate with your existing stack, whether or not Salesforce is part of it.
